# Licensing, in plain language

Status: adopted 2026-09-28 (#1806). Binding terms are in [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE), [`NOTICE`](../../NOTICE) and [`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md). This page explains them. It is not legal advice.

## Why there is a licence at all

PlayGarba was made so that Garba music is easy to find and free to listen to, for everyone. The licence is how that stays true.

Until now the repository had no licence. That did not make it free. It made it unclear, and it let someone copy the work, change the name and present it as theirs. A copyleft licence does the opposite: everything built on PlayGarba stays open, and everyone can see where it came from.

The licence adds no cost and no permission step. It asks for two things: keep it open, and give credit.

## The short version

| Part | Licence | What it means for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Code | [AGPL-3.0-only](../../LICENSE) | Use it, change it, host it. If you share or host a changed version, publish your source under the same licence. |
| Catalogue data, docs, editorial text | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) | Reuse it anywhere. Credit PlayGarba and share your changes under the same licence. |
| Name, wordmark, icons, Garbo mark | Not licensed ([`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md)) | Say "based on PlayGarba". Give your own fork its own name. |
| Music, recordings, album art, YouTube videos | Owned by their rights holders | Not ours to license. PlayGarba links to them and plays them through the official YouTube player. |

## You can

- listen, install the app and share links;
- read, study and learn from all of the code and data;
- fork the repository, change anything and run your own copy;
- use the catalogue data in research, apps, playlists or articles;
- contribute songs, corrections, fixes and features back here.

## If you share or host a changed version

1. **Keep it open.** Publish your full source under the AGPL-3.0. This also applies when people only use your version over the web (AGPL section 13).
2. **Keep the credit.** Keep [`NOTICE`](../../NOTICE), keep the copyright lines, and show `Based on PlayGarba (https://playgarba.com), source: https://github.com/ruddvz/garba` somewhere users can see it, such as an About page or footer.
3. **Say it is changed.** Do not present your version as the original PlayGarba, or as work with no connection to it.
4. **Use your own name and icon.** See [`TRADEMARKS.md`](../../TRADEMARKS.md).

These are AGPL section 7(b), 7(c) and 7(e) additional terms. They protect the credit and the name. They do not limit use.

## Contributions

Contributions come in under the same terms they go out under ("inbound = outbound"). By opening a pull request you agree that your contribution is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only for code and CC BY-SA 4.0 for data and docs, and that you have the right to submit it. Your name stays in the Git history, and you keep the copyright in what you wrote.

No contributor licence agreement is needed. Nobody can relicense your work as closed source.

## Earlier revisions

Revisions before #1806 had no licence. The copyright holders now make them available under the same terms, so a fork of an older commit has to follow them too. A copy that drops the credit or closes the source is not licensed.

## Third-party material

Songs, recordings, compositions, lyrics, album artwork, provider pages and YouTube videos belong to their owners. Catalogue records describe and link to them. They are not relicensed by PlayGarba. The redistribution policy for audio is in [`catalogue/rights.md`](../catalogue/rights.md).

## If you see a copy that breaks these terms

Open an issue with the link. The first step is always a friendly note asking them to add the credit and open their source. Most people fix it once they know.

## Evidence method

For every accepted release, the provider album page was used to verify the release identity, artist credit, release date/year, track sequence and duration. Each exact playback URL was then followed from that provider-published track list. No track IDs were constructed from neighbouring URLs, no track positions were treated as provider identifiers, and no album page is labelled as an exact-song route.

Where a traditional title appears on more than one release, the entries remain separate because the provider exposes them as distinct release recordings/medleys. This split does not silently merge recordings based on title similarity.

## Deliberately not changed in this split

- Maro Garbo and Ho Raj playback, because issue #149 owns those route clusters.
- The retired United Way 2015 placeholder-style records, which remain tombstoned in the canonical manifest.
- Additional current Atul releases surfaced during research, including 2026 catalogue material, until their full track-level provider evidence and duplicate/transliteration checks are completed in a later #151 pass.
- YouTube chapters or timestamps that were not explicitly provider-published. No timestamps were inferred from durations.

## Remaining issue #151 scope

The Atul split materially expands the foundational catalogue, but issue #151 remains open for the Hemant Chauhan and Praful Dave completeness audits, plus any remaining verified Atul gaps discovered during those follow-up passes.

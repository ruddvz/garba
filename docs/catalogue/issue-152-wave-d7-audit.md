## Provider album

Amazon Music publishes `Nortani Raat 2` on August 26, 2025 as an 11-track album credited to Kirtidan Gadhvi and Anita Pandit. The displayed track durations total 28:16.

The album is distinct from the August 29 `Nortani Raat (Garba)` split edition added in Wave D6. Both releases keep the joint primary-artist credit.

## Exact-route coverage

Exact Amazon track pages are independently verified for tracks 1-5 and 7-11.

Track 6, `Radha Ne Shyam Rame Raas Sharad Purnima Ni Raat`, is clearly present at position 6 in Amazon's official album tracklist and has a displayed duration of 2:29. However, its individual Amazon track href was not independently recoverable from the indexed provider pages.

Wave D7 therefore does not create or pattern-derive a provider ID. Track 6 remains on the release-level Amazon fallback supplied by the canonical release source.

This is intentional route truth, not incomplete bookkeeping.

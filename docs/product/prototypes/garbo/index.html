                    PlayGarba Guide

                    What is Garba?

                    The lamp

                    The word comes from garbha deep, a lamp inside a clay pot. The pot is perforated, so the light shines out through its holes. It is placed at the centre of the dance.

                    The circle

                    Dancers move around the lamp or an image of the goddess in a counterclockwise circle. The circle can grow ring by ring as more people join.

                    The steps

                    Garba is danced in named steps. Be tali has two claps and Tran tali has three. Hinch, Dodhiyu and Popatiyu each have their own movement.

                    The nights

                    Garba is danced through the nine nights of Navratri, devoted to the Mother Goddess. It is also danced at weddings and other celebrations through the year.

                    The border draws on Mata ni Pachedi, a temple cloth of Ahmedabad's Devipujak community.

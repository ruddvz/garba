## Owner decisions, 27 Sep

- **Private Garba Circle.** Call it that, make it findable, and let people in a circle play their own YouTube links, not only catalogue songs. Then merge Lives into it (owner: "merge"). **Done** in [#1760](https://github.com/ruddvz/garba/issues/1760).
- **Seek.** The ring around the garbo isn't enough, because the garbo isn't always on screen. A draggable seek bar sits under the title in Immersive. **Done** in #1757.
- **Faces.** A face with a transparent background (PNG or WebP) is worn like the singers' cut-out heads, with no circle or ring. A normal photo is fitted into a circle first. **Done** in #1757.
- **Names.** Up to 10 characters, always on one line. **Done** in #1757.
- **Immersive Explore.** Opens beside the DJ like search, shows only artist essentials and step playlists, plays only that artist or playlist, and marks songs that can't play **Not available**. It has to hold up when a phone runs the music for a whole ground during Navratri. **Done** in [#1761](https://github.com/ruddvz/garba/issues/1761): Explore now opens on Steps and styles and Artist essentials. Playing a list keeps Next, auto-play and shuffle on it, and it's still on after a reload. A video YouTube refuses is marked **Not available** and skipped, and the next songs are checked while one plays.
- **Explore collections.** Bigger Tran Taali, Be Taali, Dakla, Dodhiyu and Hinch collections with verified songs. [#1762](https://github.com/ruddvz/garba/issues/1762).
- **Background play.** Point people to Brave on phones and iPad, or to the installed app. Any browser works on a laptop.
- **Couple garba.** Undecided. The couple already dances in Immersive. Leave it as is until there's a clearer ask.

## Can't find it: why, and what to do

### Garba Circle

Devanshi asked for "a feature like Spotify in which we can create our private group of friends and listen to same garba in that group. No outside members and we can play whatever we want."

**What Garba Circle does today** (`docs/product/garba-circle.md`): one person starts a circle and shares a link or QR code. Everyone who opens it hears the same song at the same moment, each on their own phone. Only people with the link can join, and there's no public list. That covers "private group" and "listen to the same garba".

**What it doesn't do:** "we can play whatever we want". A circle plays one shared order: the host's current song first, then the catalogue shuffled with the circle's seed. Next and Previous explain that the circle moves together. Nobody picks the next song.

**Why people don't find it:**

1. On phones and iPad (under 1024 px), the topbar hides the Circle button. It lives only inside **More**, as a row that just says "Garba Circle" with no description (`styles/60-runtime-and-provider.css`, "The top bar keeps only what each screen needs").
2. On desktop it's an icon with no label, a ring of dots. The tooltip ("listen together in sync") doesn't exist on touch screens.
3. "Circle" means something else everywhere else in the product. In Immersive, **View → Where you stand → In the circle** is the dance circle, and the Sound card calls itself "The circle around the song". Someone looking for "listen with friends" has no reason to tap a word that already means the dance.
4. In Immersive the tile is **More → Garba Circle, "Open the listening circle"**. That describes the name, not the use.

**Recommendation.** Don't build a second group-listening feature yet. Say what it does next to the name everywhere:

- More row (Simple) and More tile (Immersive): **Garba Circle**, with the line "Listen together with friends" under it. The name stays, and the line says what it's for.
- Topbar button `aria-label`/`title`: "Listen with friends (Garba Circle)".
- How to use and FAQ pages: a short "Can I listen with friends?" answer that points to it.
- Tell Devanshi it exists. The form has no reply channel, so a line on the Tally thank-you screen, "Want to listen with friends? Try Garba Circle in More", reaches everyone who asks.

**Done in #1760:** it's called **Private Garba Circle** everywhere. A chip above 24/7 LIVE reads "Private Garba Circle · Listen with friends" before a circle and carries the host's face and name during one. Immersive has a **Circle** button in its rail. The dialog opens over Immersive instead of switching to Simple. How to use has a "Listen with friends" section (the FAQ is held by #847).

**The gap.** Letting the host choose the songs is the "play whatever we want" part. Immersive has a prototype of this, **More → Lives**: a host picks an avatar, a name and a playlist, and shares a link. Lives computes the position from the device clock, and its own code says the real player would use Live Radio's server-aligned clock (`docs/product/prototypes/garbo/garbo.js`, "hosted lives"). Its share link points at the prototype page. So it isn't ready to promote as is. #1754 has landed (host face and name). The owner decided that people in a circle can add their own YouTube links, which the link carries the way Lives carries its playlist. **Done in #1760:** the owner chose to merge Lives into the circle. A circle now starts with **Keep the music going** (today's shared shuffle) or **Play your songs** (this song, Up next and pasted YouTube links, up to 30, carried in the link). The host adds more at the end and shares the new link. Lives' tile left the embedded player. Details: `docs/product/garba-circle.md`.

### Seek (Harsh: "not able to fast forward"; smit: "forward and backward option")

- **Simple:** the thin progress bar under the song is a seek slider (`#progress`). It stays disabled until YouTube reports the length, so for the first second or two after tapping a song it doesn't respond (`assets/runtime/seek-state.js`).
- **Immersive:** seeking is the bar under the song title. The invisible ring around the garbo, which nothing marked as draggable, is gone (#1781).
- Lock-screen and headset controls already support seek and ±10 s where the phone offers them (`youtube-player-runtime.js`, Media Session).
- "Forward and backward" may also mean Next and Previous, which exist in both players.

**Done (#1757):** a slim draggable seek bar between the elapsed and total time. It drives the ring's own seek, so Live, chapters and the embedded player behave as before, and it hides on Live. On a laptop, J and L already skip 10 seconds.

### Background play (Mayursinh, Jeet)

On iPhone, iPad and most Android phones, YouTube stops when the browser goes to the background or the screen locks. A web page can't keep it playing, and PlayGarba doesn't work around it (`docs/product/my-songs.md`).

**Owner's answer for listeners:** on phones and iPad, use Brave, which has its own background playback for web video, or install PlayGarba as an app. On a laptop any browser keeps playing.

**To verify before it goes on the FAQ:** that the installed app keeps YouTube playing with the screen locked on iPhone and iPad. iOS pauses embedded video in a backgrounded web app, so this may only hold on Android. Test it on a real device first. **Status: FAQ answer, once verified.**

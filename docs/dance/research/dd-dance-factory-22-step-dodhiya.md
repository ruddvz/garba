## Motion-design observations

The sampled footage offers several distinct pose references: hands behind the lower back during travel; wide and offset stances; profile and back-facing orientations; a curved overhead arm paired with a lower or forward arm; forward palm projections; low diagonal reaches; and crossing-looking travel. These remain source-specific visual motifs. Do not label them as canonical Garba vocabulary or convert them into 22 numbered animation units without a complete event transcription and practitioner review.

Still samples cannot establish which anatomical foot moves, heel/toe contact, weight transfer, hand-to-foot timing, clap contact, circle path, or the song’s beat grid. The page provides no caption track or visible chapter list, and this review did not transcribe speech. A Garba practitioner and a source-native count/translation pass are needed before making the sequence animation-ready.

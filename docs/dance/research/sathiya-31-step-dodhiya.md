## What this review can and cannot say

The title says “31 steps Dodhiya.” I did not find legible on-screen step numbers
or transcript evidence that identifies 31 separately named movement units. In
this note, **31-step** is retained only as the publisher's title. It is not
treated as 31 beats, 31 bars, or a confirmed complete inventory of 31 unique
traditional steps.

The video was checked in the YouTube player at normal speed using sparse
checkpoints distributed over its full timeline. The checkpoint log was written
to [research issue #2257](https://github.com/ruddvz/garba/issues/2257). Some
rapid seeks rendered a frame a few seconds behind the displayed seek time, so
times below are approximate ranges. The pass is not frame-by-frame: it cannot
establish every foot contact, the complete order, left/right lead, clap contact,
or beat placement.

## Provisional motion motifs

These are names for shapes observed in this video review, not canonical step
labels:

- wide lateral lunge with arms opening;
- high/low or cross-body arm sweep;
- knee lift with raised arm;
- forward/crossing travel with chest-level hands;
- back lean with upward reach;
- traveling side step with curved arms.

The footage alternates instruction, held poses, brief group practice and reset.
Do not animate the complete 20-minute upload as one continuous routine. The
motifs are pose/movement research leads only; this pass does not establish how
they repeat or connect.

**Recommendation finding:** Sathiya Garba International / Hiren Patel is the
strongest single-channel archive lead in the PlayGarba comparison so far: its
channel shelf separates multiple counted Dodhiya/Rangat lessons, hand-movement
lessons and named-form playlists. That is an archive-breadth finding, not proof
that one teacher covers every Garba tradition. The reviewed Reddit discussions
do not establish one recommended creator; replies instead include learning in
a live Garba circle or starting with Ek/Be/Tran Taali. The logged-out Sathiya
Instagram profile did not expose Reel content or current follower/like metrics
in this pass. See the dated [cross-platform comparison](../sources.md) for its
small, self-selected Reddit sample, Instagram access limit and other creator
comparisons.

## Beat-sync and motion-data handoff

No event in this sparse pass has a defensible beat index, beat phase, phrase
length or loop boundary. Do not fill one from the “31-step” title or infer BPM

laterality, clap/hand contact and turn direction remain unresolved. Keep these

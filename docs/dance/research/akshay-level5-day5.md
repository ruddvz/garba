## Source-supported motion vocabulary

The samples support investigating these visual motifs for a later continuous review:

- **Feet and legs:** feet together or close; offset/crossing-looking placements; bent knees; brief lifted-foot shapes; a wide stance and lateral leg reach.
- **Hands and arms:** relaxed low arms; open outward palms; arms open to the sides; a high arm paired with a forearm across the chest.
- **Torso and facing:** front, side and back-facing samples; a lateral lean.
- **Travel:** one brief side-travel sample with a trailing lifted foot.

These are pose-level observations. The samples do not establish how the motifs connect, repeat, or form one phrase. The course label “Level 5 / Day 5” identifies a lesson in the creator’s sequence; it does not name a Garba form, step number, or beat count.

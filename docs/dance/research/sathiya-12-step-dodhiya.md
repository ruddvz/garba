## Movement candidates and unknowns

At 5:57, the teacher and several learners are visibly in a soft-kneed offset/crossing-looking reach with low or behind-the-back arms; the 5:47 and 6:04 samples bracket that image with instruction/reset poses. At 7:27 the teacher demonstrates another lateral reach while learners wait; 7:34 shows a front-facing explanation. At 8:59, the teacher holds a separate side-facing diagonal pose with one arm raised while the learners watch. The 10:59, 11:09 and 11:17 samples show the teacher move from a broad front-facing stance to an overhead arm shape and then a forward, asymmetric reach while the learners watch. Because the frames are sparse, they do not prove this is one continuous step sequence. The 18:00–18:04 sample is another instruction/demo moment with the teacher's hands in front. These are visual candidates only; do not label them as named traditional steps from this video alone.

The samples do not establish step-by-step boundaries, a twelve-item enumeration, lead/support foot, heel or toe contacts, weight-transfer order, hand contacts, travel around a circle, facing changes through a full cycle, or teacher count cues. Captions were unavailable, and no score was derived from the audio.

# Source-specific Garba tutorial reviews

This notebook records what can be verified in individual lessons before any movement is turned into a character animation. The companion [`routine-reviews.json`](../../data/dance/routine-reviews.json) keeps the observations machine-readable and links each review to a known catalogue record where possible; a shared video may instead name related candidate records when its sections cannot be mapped individually.

## Review rules

- Keep the teacher, video, title and local spelling attached to every sequence. A tutorial title is not proof of a universal Garba step or regional standard.
- Keep a video's advertised number of steps separate from beats, claps and phrase length. Do not infer a tempo grid from editing, lyrics or a few sampled frames.
- Describe visible positions and travel only at timestamps that were actually inspected. These are sparse review points, not full continuous-motion transcriptions.
- Mark left/right as unknown if camera mirroring, facing direction, clothing or frame sampling prevents reliable identification. Say whose right is meant when a teacher states it.
- Auto-generated captions can help navigate to an explanation. They are not a dependable source for counts, foot placement or movement names without listening and visual confirmation.
- `animationScore` stays `null` until the full routine has a beat-indexed foot, hand, facing, travel and phrase-boundary transcription, then receives practitioner review.

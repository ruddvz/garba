START HERE

From landing to listening in three moves.

You do not need to learn a separate system. Open the listening app, choose the kind of Garba you want, then play or browse.

01 · Open

Go to the live player.

Use live.playgarba.com whenever you want to listen. playgarba.com remains the public guide and discovery site.

02 · Choose

Pick your Garba world.

Move between Traditional, Dandiya, Devotional, Folk, Sanedo and Fusion. On phones, the genre strip scrolls horizontally.

03 · Play

Stay with one track or keep going.

Use the main player for individual tracks, open Browse to find something specific, or move into Nonstop for longer continuous sets.

01 · THE TWO ADDRESSES

Website outside. Player inside.

playgarba.com is the public home for learning what PlayGarba is, understanding how it works and finding the right entry point.

live.playgarba.com is the listening application. That is the address to bookmark, install on your home screen and use for day-to-day playback.

03 · BROWSE AND SEARCH

Use Browse when you already have something in mind.

Open Browse Songs to move beyond the current track. Search starts with title and artist matching, then the catalogue can use Garba categories, styles and aliases to help with discovery when an ordinary match is not enough.

On phones, Browse opens as a bottom sheet. You can expand or collapse it without losing the player underneath. On larger screens, it becomes a centred browsing panel.

04 · PLAYER CONTROLS

The controls stay in the same place while the music changes.

The main listening controls are built to remain geometrically stable when titles, genres and artwork change. Depending on the available playback source, you can use play and pause, previous and next, seeking, favourites, share and queue controls.

Play / pauseStart or stop the current source from the central player control.
Previous / nextMove through the active listening context without reopening Browse.
FavouriteKeep a track close from the current-track area.
QueueSee and manage what is lined up when the current context exposes a queue.
Lock screen and media controls

Where the browser exposes Media Session controls, PlayGarba can surface play, pause, previous, next and supported seek actions through the operating system media controls.

05 · NONSTOP

For nights when one track at a time is the wrong unit.

Nonstop is the dedicated route for longer Garba sets and continuous performances. It lives inside the same listening application, so you can move there without learning a second interface.

06 · PLAYBACK SOURCES

The catalogue and the audio source are not always the same thing.

PlayGarba can resolve a catalogue item to different approved playback routes, including direct audio and cited external providers. That means the exact playback surface can vary from one item to another.

If a track relies on an external provider, availability, advertising, sign-in requirements and provider-specific controls are governed by that provider. PlayGarba keeps the catalogue and listening context together, but it cannot remove a provider's own rules.

07 · OFFLINE

The app shell can survive a lost connection. Streaming media does not become a download.

After a successful online visit, the PlayGarba web app can cache its interface, lightweight artwork fallbacks and previously requested static assets. If the network disappears, the cached shell or offline page can still open.

External provider audio and video are not silently downloaded for offline listening. You should expect an internet connection to be required for normal music playback.

Useful distinction

Installing PlayGarba makes the website behave more like an app. It does not turn the catalogue into an offline music library.

08 · QUICK ANSWERS

A few things worth knowing.

Do I need an App Store or Play Store download?

No. PlayGarba is a progressive web app. Open the live player in a supported browser and install or add it to your home screen. See the dedicated install guide for device-specific steps.

Which URL should I save?

Save live.playgarba.com for listening. Use playgarba.com for the public site, guides and general information.

Can I use PlayGarba without installing it?

Yes. The live player works as a normal website. Installation simply gives you a home-screen or desktop app entry point and a standalone app-style window where supported.

Does installing it make songs available offline?

No. The interface can cache enough to recover more gracefully when the network is unavailable, but streaming media is not automatically downloaded for offline use.

Why can two songs behave differently?

Different catalogue items can resolve to different playback sources. External providers can have their own availability, sign-in, advertising and control behaviour.

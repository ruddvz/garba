# PlayGarba name and brand

The code is free under the AGPL-3.0 and the catalogue data is free under CC BY-SA 4.0. The name is the one thing that is not shared, and that protects everyone: a listener who opens something called PlayGarba should get PlayGarba, not a copy someone else has changed.

## What is covered

- the name **PlayGarba** and the domain `playgarba.com`;
- the PlayGarba wordmark and the app icons in `assets/icons/`;
- the Garbo mark (`public-site/assets/garbo-mark.svg`);
- names or logos that are confusingly close to these.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is **based on PlayGarba**, forked from PlayGarba or compatible with PlayGarba. The attribution line in [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) is required, not just allowed.
- Use the name when you write, talk or teach about the project.
- Run PlayGarba unchanged for yourself, a class, a community event or a private group.
- Open issues and pull requests here. Contributions keep the name, because they land in PlayGarba.

## What needs a different name

A public fork or deployment that changes the code, the catalogue or the design must:

- use its own name, icon and domain;
- keep the attribution required by [`NOTICE`](NOTICE);
- not describe itself as the official PlayGarba, or as a project with no connection to PlayGarba.

This is not about stopping forks. Fork freely. Just give it your own name and say where it came from.

## Asking for permission

For anything not covered here, open an issue at [github.com/ruddvz/garba/issues](https://github.com/ruddvz/garba/issues).

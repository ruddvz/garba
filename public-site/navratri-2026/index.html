01 · DATES

When is Navratri 2026?

Incredible India's national Navratri listing gives Sunday, 11 October through Monday, 19 October 2026 for the nine-night festival.

Gujarat Tourism's state-sponsored Vibrant Navratri Festival in Ahmedabad is separately listed from 11 through 20 October 2026. PlayGarba keeps those two official date ranges separate rather than treating them as the same event.

03 · VIBRANT NAVRATRI

Vibrant Navratri Festival 2026: GMDC Ground, Ahmedabad.

Incredible India lists the Gujarat Tourism-hosted Vibrant Navratri Festival at GMDC Ground in Ahmedabad from 11 to 20 October 2026. Its current listing includes nightly Maha Aarti, cultural programming, a handicraft bazaar and an opening-night performance involving more than 1,000 artists.

Event programmes can change. Check the official tourism listing before making travel or attendance plans.

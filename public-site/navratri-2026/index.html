NAVRATRI 2026

Garba season starts in October.

Official tourism listings place Navratri from 11 to 19 October 2026, with Gujarat's Vibrant Navratri Festival in Ahmedabad listed from 11 to 20 October.

01 · DATES

Navratri 2026: 11–19 October.

Incredible India's national Navratri listing gives Sunday, 11 October through Monday, 19 October 2026 for the nine-night festival.

A separate official listing for Gujarat's Vibrant Navratri Festival runs from 11 through 20 October 2026. These are separate listings, so PlayGarba keeps both date ranges explicit rather than merging them into one claim.

02 · VIBRANT NAVRATRI

GMDC Ground, Ahmedabad: 11–20 October 2026.

Incredible India lists the Vibrant Navratri Festival 2026 at GMDC Ground in Ahmedabad, hosted by Gujarat Tourism. The current listing describes nightly celebrations, Maha Aarti, cultural programming, a handicraft bazaar and an opening-night performance involving more than 1,000 artists.

Event details can change, so check the official tourism listing before making travel or ticket plans.

03 · LISTEN

Build your Garba listening around the kind of night you want.

Use Traditional for core Garba, Devotional for the Navratri worship context, Dandiya for Raas-led listening, or Nonstop for longer continuous sets.

PlayGarba uses YouTube as the playback source and keeps the catalogue organised around Garba rather than general music.

04 · BEFORE NAVRATRI

Know what you are dancing.

Start with What is Garba?, then read Navratri and Garba, Dandiya Raas and the garbo and garbha-deep.

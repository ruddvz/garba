PLAYER GUIDE

How to use PlayGarba.

Open the player, choose the kind of Garba you want and press play. The rest is optional.

Open player ↗ Install PlayGarba →

01 · START

Choose a sound, then listen.

Use live.playgarba.com for listening. Start with Traditional, Dandiya, Devotional, Folk, Sanedo or Fusion. If you already know the song or artist you want, open Explore instead.

https://live.playgarba.com/ Open ↗

02 · CONTROLS

Keep the controls close to the music.

Play and pause stay central. Previous and next move through the current listening context. Favourites, share and queue appear where they are useful.

Open Explore catalogue ↗

03 · NONSTOP

For longer Garba sets.

Nonstop is for continuous releases and longer performances. It uses the same player, so you can move in and out without learning another interface.

Open Nonstop ↗

04 · YOUTUBE

Playback comes from YouTube.

PlayGarba uses verified YouTube routes for playback. The video stays collapsed unless you open it. If a catalogue item does not yet have a verified YouTube route, it can remain unavailable until one is added.
